# Matthew Scriv — public-information analysis

**Research date:** 2026-08-14  
**Scope:** public material only. No paid course, private chat, reverse engineering,
or claim of access to proprietary rules.

## Bottom line

The available public evidence is too thin to establish either Matthew Scriv's
complete method or his performance. His own public clips support only a few
high-level observations: he describes marking levels in advance, shows explicit
entries/exits, says he does not use trend lines, and presents a repeatable “model”
to students. Screenshots and self-reported dollar profits are not an audited
track record. Anonymous discussion that labels the model “Chrome,” OTE/IFVG, or
ICT/SMC influenced is **unverified Tier 5 commentary**, not a primary source.

Accordingly Serein does not copy or attribute proprietary rules. It translates
only generic, testable hypotheses—level breach, failure/re-entry, higher-timeframe
context, structural invalidation, and asymmetric target—then subjects them to the
same OOS and cost tests as every other candidate.

## Decision

- **Keep as hypotheses:** predeclared levels, structural invalidation, failed
  breakout/re-entry, and selective continuation after pullback.
- **Reject as evidence:** screenshots, isolated winners, anonymous descriptions,
  and unexplained “liquidity/smart-money” terminology.
- **Unknown:** full model, win rate, expectancy, leverage, drawdown, execution,
  account aggregation, and audited long-run performance.
